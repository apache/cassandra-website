Events

Cassandra events bring the community together. Everyone (whether they are an individual user, contributor, or company) is welcome to attend and help organize these events. They are an opportunity for users, enthusiasts, and community members to share their experiences working with Cassandra daily, hear talks and participate whether they are beginners or experts.

Organizing an event or want to get involved? Take a look at the Meetup Organizer Handbook for guidelines to getting started and make sure you that you double check the Events Approval Checklist for compliance. Any other questions? Get in touch with us!

Not every event below is run by the Apache Software Foundation. Community Over Code is the ASF's own conference; user group meetups, workshops and vendor webinars are organized independently and are listed here to support the community. Where an event is run by a company rather than the project, the card says so.
